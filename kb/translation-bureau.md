# Knowledge base — "FromToBridge" translation bureau (fictional, demo only)

All names, prices, and policies below are invented for a demo. Not a real
company.

## Services

- Written translation (general, technical, legal, medical, marketing)
- Certified translation (with the bureau's stamp and translator's signature)
- Notarized translation (notary certifies the translator's signature)
- Sworn translation for EU authorities (via partner sworn translators in DE, PL)
- Proofreading and editing of existing translations
- Website and software localization
- Apostille assistance (document routed to the issuing authority)

## Language pairs

Core: Ukrainian ⇄ English, German, Polish, French, Spanish, Italian.
Via relay: most European pairs. Rare pairs quoted case by case.

## Turnaround (business days, standard tier)

- up to 10 pages: 2–3 days
- 11–50 pages: 4–7 days
- 50+ pages: quoted per project
- Rush (next business day / same day): +50% / +100% surcharge, subject to
  translator availability

One standard page = 1800 characters with spaces.

## Indicative pricing (EUR per standard page, ex. VAT)

- General: 12–16
- Technical / legal / medical: 18–24
- Certified: + 6 per document
- Notarization: + 20 per document (notary fee included)
- Minimum order: 1 page

Exact price depends on pair, subject matter, volume, and deadline. The
assistant must never commit to a final figure — it collects parameters and
tells the client a manager will confirm the quote.

## Certification vs notarization

- **Certified**: the bureau confirms the translation is complete and accurate.
  Accepted by most universities, employers, and many consulates.
- **Notarized**: a notary certifies the identity and signature of the
  translator. Required by courts, migration offices, and civil registry
  offices in most countries.
- If the client is unsure, ask which authority will receive the document.

## Delivery

- Original hard copy with wet stamp: courier (Nova Poshta within Ukraine,
  DHL abroad), 1–3 days, cost at carrier tariff
- Pickup from the Kyiv office: by appointment

a human manager during office hours; outside them it collects the request and
promises a manager reply the next business morning.
