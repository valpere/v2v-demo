# Knowledge base — "FromToBridge" translation bureau (fictional, demo only)

All names, prices, and policies below are invented for a demo. Not a real
company. Figures are plausible for the Kyiv market (2026) but not taken from
any real bureau.

FromToBridge is a Kyiv bureau working mostly with private clients and
companies in Germany, Austria, Poland and other EU countries, so quotes are
given in EUR.

## Services

- Written translation — general, legal, technical, medical, financial,
  marketing, academic
- Certified translation — the bureau's stamp and the translator's signature
  confirming the translation is complete and accurate
- Notarized translation — a notary certifies the translator's identity and
  signature
- Sworn translation — via partner sworn translators, for **German, Polish,
  Italian, French, Czech** only
- Apostille and consular legalisation assistance
- Proofreading and editing of an existing translation
- Website and software localisation
- Interpreting — consecutive and simultaneous (with equipment)

## How a price is formed

The price depends on the **language pair**, the **subject area**, the
**volume**, and the **deadline**. A manager sends a firm quote after seeing
the document — usually within 15 minutes during office hours.

One **standard page = 1800 characters including spaces**.

## Turnaround (business days, standard tier)

- up to 10 pages: **2–3 days**
- 11–50 pages: **4–7 days**
- 50+ pages: quoted per project

- Standard single document (passport, certificate, diploma): often **1

## Certification vs notarization vs sworn

- **Certified** — the bureau confirms the translation is complete and
  accurate with its stamp and the translator's signature. Accepted by most
  universities, employers, and many consulates.
- **Notarized** — a notary certifies the translator's signature. Required by
  courts, migration offices, and civil-registry offices in most countries.
- **Sworn** — done and stamped by a court-sworn translator in the target
  country. Required by some German and Polish authorities that do not accept a
  Ukrainian notarization. Available for DE, PL, IT, FR, CZ only.
- If the client is unsure, ask **which authority will receive the document
  and in which country** — that decides which level is needed.

## Delivery

- Scanned PDF by email — included, same day as completion
- Original hard copy with wet stamp — courier (Nova Poshta in Ukraine, DHL
  abroad), 1–3 days, at the carrier's tariff
- Pickup from the Kyiv office — by appointment
- Free courier within Ukraine for orders over EUR 150

## Payment

Bank transfer with or without VAT, card, Privat24, EUR/USD wire, and
cryptocurrency. For companies: monthly invoicing and a contract with fixed
KPIs and deadlines.

## Confidentiality

Every order is covered by a confidentiality clause; a separate NDA is signed
on request. Client documents are used only to carry out the order, are stored
for **6 months** after completion and then deleted, and are never shared with
third parties except the assigned translator, editor, and (if ordered) the
notary. Disclosure carries liability under Ukrainian law.

## Quality process

Every translation goes through **translator → editor → subject-area
specialist** before delivery.

## Working hours

Office: Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 EET. The assistant works 24/7 and hands off to a
human manager during office hours; outside them it collects the request and
promises a manager reply the next business morning.

## Escalate to a human when

- the client asks for a person or is unhappy with the assistant
- sworn translation is needed for a language other than DE/PL/IT/FR/CZ
- legal / liability questions (contracts, court deadlines, admissibility of a
  translation for a specific authority)
- complaints about a delivered translation
- payment disputes or refund requests
- interpreting bookings (date, venue, equipment, number of interpreters)
- anything not covered above
